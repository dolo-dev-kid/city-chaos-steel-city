HOW TO PLAY

Move / Drive
WASD + Shift. E enters the nearest car. Space is jump or handbrake.

Fight
Mouse aims. Left-click shoots. Right-click holds ADS. R reloads. 1 / 2 / 3 switch weapons.

Survive
Gang members shoot back. Police chase when Wanted rises. Hide for 7 seconds to cool down.

Home Base
St. Clair Village is the hub — you spawn here. The projects are on the map: Hill District, Arlington Heights, Beltzhoover, Garfield, Wilkinsburg, East Liberty, Swissvale, Clairton. Each one has cars to grab and work to do.

FIRST 60 SECONDS

Grab the car near you in St. Clair Village → follow the yellow checkpoint south to Duquesne → then take the fight to the projects.

On touch devices: left joystick + SHOOT / JUMP / CAR buttons.
